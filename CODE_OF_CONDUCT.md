# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Grounded DocParse community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of personal identity,
background, appearance, ability, experience, education, or beliefs. We will
act in ways that support an open, welcoming, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- communicate with empathy and respect;
- welcome different viewpoints and technical experience levels;
- give and receive constructive, specific feedback;
- acknowledge mistakes, repair harm where possible, and learn from them; and
- prioritize the safety and health of the whole community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized conduct,
personal attacks, trolling, deliberate intimidation, publishing private
information without permission, or other conduct that would be inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, commits,
reviews, wiki content, project events, and public spaces where someone is
officially representing Grounded DocParse.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[GitHub Security Advisory form](https://github.com/pypi-ahmad/grounded-docparse/security/advisories/new).
Start the report title with `Conduct report`. Do not open a public issue or
include unnecessary personal data.

Project maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, protect reporter
privacy, and disclose information only as needed to investigate and respond.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue a private correction or
warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation
when necessary. Anyone with a conflict of interest must not take part in the
decision.
